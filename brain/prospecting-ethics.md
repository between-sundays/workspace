# Prospecting — the rules we hold ourselves to

We do outreach the way a good B2B team does: research first, one person at a time,
a real reason for the message. That only works if people feel *noticed*, not
*harvested*. These are the constraints. They are not optional, and they are as
much about the brand as the law.

## Record signals, not verdicts

Store **what someone publicly posted** — "shared a photo from their church's
Wednesday night group" — and never a stored conclusion about who they are.

Religious belief is a **special category of personal data** under GDPR/UK GDPR,
and several US state laws treat it as sensitive too. A spreadsheet column that
amounts to "this named person is a Christian" is a compliance problem and, more
to the point, is not who we are. The `signal` field on every prospect exists so
the observable fact is what gets kept.

## Public, and in the open

- Only material a person published publicly, viewed the ordinary way.
- **No scraping tools against LinkedIn.** Automated collection breaks its terms
  and gets accounts restricted. Search and read like a person does.
- No buying lists, no email-finding services, no scraped contact databases.
- Nothing behind a login that isn't ours, no private groups under false pretenses.

## Approach like a person

- Every message references something specific and real about *them*. If we can't
  say why we're writing, we're not ready to write.
- One message. A second only if there's a genuine new reason. Never a sequence.
- Say who we are and what Between Sundays is, in the first message.
- If someone asks to be left alone, they're removed and never re-added. Log it as
  `passed` — that's a permanent no, not a "later".
- Personal data goes in the private store only, and never into a URL or a page.

## The Source Rule applies here too

Every persona carries at least one named verse. Not as decoration on a sales
document — if we can't say what part of scripture makes this person's situation
one we have something for, we don't understand them well enough to write to them.

## Where this ends up

This list is a **staging table, not a CRM**. When a real CRM lands, it exports and
this stops being the system of record. Build nothing here that a CRM does better.
