# The Between Sundays Brand Constitution
*Source: Adrian, 2026-08-08. This document outranks style preferences. Read it before touching a page.*

## Who we are
- We are **messengers**, not experts. **The friend, not the teacher.**
- We are going on the journey too. We can be travel guides because we're not
  clueless — but we don't know it all, and we never pretend to.
- We build this for **our own journey as believers**. Much of what we publish is
  literally *things we are learning*.
- We are not Bible scholars. **But we do know God. And we want to know him
  better. We want to make him proud. And we want other people to know him like
  we do.**

## Why this exists (the founder's origin, in substance)
Adrian never learned to like reading. School taught him to work the system, not
to learn. He wants to *just get in the Word and understand* — and there have to
be more ways in than the scholarly way. There are. This paper is one of them.
Every page should be **another door into the same book.**

## The Source Rule (hard rule)
> Everything created in the paper — full-page design or lengthy reading page —
> must be tied back to **at least one named Bible verse, printed on the page.**
> If it's Bible-sourced, we cannot be wrong.

Enforced by `tools/check_sourced.py` in CI. But note: **attaching a verse does
not make a claim correct.** The verse must actually support what the page says —
context-fit is reviewed by humans and agents (`SCRIPTURE` flags), never auto-passed.

## Voice & craft
- **Never preachy.** Share what's there, clear as day. Notice, point, hand over.
- **5th-grade reading level** (verify with an FK score, don't guess).
- **Primary translations: MSG and NLT** (easy to read); WEB for bulk/public-domain
  needs. Always *fetch* verse text — never quote from memory. Attribution notices
  live on page 02.
- **Modern, never vintage-for-vintage's-sake. No depictions of Bible characters.**
- **Print-first.** Coupons, tear-outs, folios, halftones — never web furniture.
- **Newspaper structure first, creativity layered inside it.**
- No fabricated testimony, statistics, or bylines. Real people only.
