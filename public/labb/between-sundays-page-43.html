Yahweh answered me,
“Write the vision, and make it
plain on tablets, that he who
runs may read it.
For the vision is yet for the
appointed time, and it hurries toward
the end, and won’t prove false.
Though it takes time, wait for it;
because it will surely come.
It won’t delay.”
Habakkuk 2 : 2–3 · that is the whole assignment
Write one specific thing you believe
God has put in front of you.
Not a feeling. A thing.
With a size and a date on it.
Then put this page where you will see it.
It may take longer than you want. That is not the same as late.
