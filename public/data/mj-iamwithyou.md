# I AM WITH YOU — Midjourney direction

Six plates. Generate each one **without any text**. Every label, every verse,
every word gets set by us in layout.

---

## PLATE 1 — the crosswalk (the hero, full page)

```
aerial photograph looking straight down at a busy city crosswalk, several dozen
ordinary people walking in many directions, seen from directly overhead, tops of
heads and shoulders, everyday clothing in muted colours with a few strong reds
and yellows, wide gaps of empty grey asphalt between the groups, worn white
crosswalk paint, damp pavement, flat overcast daylight, no hard shadows, shot
from a sixth-floor window with a 35mm lens, documentary, unposed
--ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7
```

**Why:** the gaps are the design. Every empty patch of asphalt is where a label
goes. Overcast light means no shadow fights the type.

## PLATE 2 — the plaza from a balcony

```
high angle photograph of a European stone plaza in late afternoon, people walking
alone and in pairs across pale worn paving, long thin shadows, cafe tables at the
edges, seen from a second-floor balcony at roughly forty degrees, 50mm,
unposed candid documentary photography, muted natural colour
--ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7
```

**Why:** a second angle stops the spread feeling like one dataset. The forty-
degree view reads as human rather than surveillance.

## PLATE 3 — the train platform

## PLATE 6 — the stairwell (the closing image)

```
photograph looking down a concrete apartment stairwell from the top floor,
empty, worn steps spiralling away, single fluorescent light, institutional paint,
```

**Why:** the page ends on an empty place. The promise was made to a man alone in

## If a plate comes back wrong

- People too clustered → add *"wide gaps of empty pavement between groups"*
- Too stylised or cinematic → make sure `--style raw` is there, remove any
  adjective like *cinematic, dramatic, epic*
- Faces recognisable → add *"seen from above, faces not visible"*
- Light too pretty → replace with *"flat overcast, no hard shadows"*
- It invents signage or lettering → add `--no text, letters, signs, words`
