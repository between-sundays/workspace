What we hand a printer

Two halves: what is settled and verified in the build system, and what is still an open question. Nothing in the second half has been decided, and nothing there should be quoted to a vendor as if it had been.

Settled — verified in the build
ProductBroadsheet newspaper, 48 pages, Issue 001 — I Am With You
Trim size332 × 475 mm portrait
Build canvas941 × 1346 px at 96 dpi (the CSS page box every builder targets)
Review renders1295 × 1852 px — screen only, never for print
Print target3921 × 5610 px = 300 dpi at trim
Module systemFull page · half landscape (941 × 673) · half portrait (470 × 1346) · quarter (470 × 673). Every element is built to one of these.
ColourFull colour throughout. Department spot inks are part of the design, not an extra.
Special constructionThe Spine — a removable inner section (pages 23–26). Needs to be liftable by a reader without tearing the rest.
Source ruleEvery page carries at least one named Bible verse, printed. Checked in CI.
Open — do not quote these as decisions
Unanswered
  1. Paper stock and weight. Not chosen. Newsprint, improved newsprint and uncoated all read very differently for halftones — needs samples in hand, not a spec sheet.
  2. Printer. No vendor selected, no quotes gathered. Who can run 332 × 475 mm?
  3. Run length. Undecided, and it drives everything downstream.
  4. Bleed and safe zone. The build has no bleed allowance yet — the printer's spec decides this, and every page needs re-rendering once it's known.
  5. File format. Presumably press-ready PDF/X, but the printer's requirement governs.
  6. Binding / folding. How the Spine detaches cleanly is a production question.
The known blocker
Every placed artwork is currently around 80 dpi at trim. Print needs 3921 × 5610. Until the source art is re-exported at full resolution, there is no printable PDF — no matter how finished the pages look on screen.
