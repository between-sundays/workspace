Who we're going after

B2B discipline pointed at a B2C audience. Not "our audience is Christians" — that's not a target, it's a census. These are specific collections of people, each with the public signals that find them, the angle that fits their actual situation, and the verse that says why we have something for them. Then we go one by one.

The rules we work under

We record what someone publicly posted, never a stored verdict about who they are. No scraping tools against LinkedIn, no bought lists, no message sequences. Every note is something they chose to publish. The full standard is in the prospecting rules — worth reading before anyone sends anything.
