The constitution

What We Believe

This outranks style preferences, taste, and anybody's good idea — mine included. If a page conflicts with what's on this sheet, the page is wrong.

Who we are
We are messengers, not experts. The friend, not the teacher. We're going on the journey too.
  • We can be travel guides because we're not clueless — but we don't know it all, and we never pretend to.
  • We build this for our own journey as believers. Much of what we publish is literally things we are learning.
  • We are not Bible scholars. But we do know God. We want to know him better, we want to make him proud, and we want other people to know him like we do.
Why this exists

Adrian never learned to like reading. School taught him to work the system, not to learn. He wants to just get in the Word and understand — and there have to be more ways in than the scholarly way. There are. This paper is one of them.

Every page should be another door into the same book.
The Source Rule — hard rule
Everything created in the paper must be tied back to at least one named Bible verse, printed on the page. If it's Bible-sourced, we cannot be wrong.

Enforced on every push by tools/check_sourced.py. But a verse stapled to a page does not make the page true — the verse has to actually support what the page says. Context-fit is reviewed by people and agents with a SCRIPTURE flag, and is never auto-passed.

Voice & craft
  • Never preachy. Share what's there, clear as day. Notice, point, hand over.
  • 5th-grade reading level — verify with a score, don't guess.
  • MSG and NLT first for readability; WEB where we need public domain. Always fetch the verse text, never quote from memory. Attributions live on page 02.
  • Modern, never vintage for its own sake. No depictions of Bible characters.
  • Print-first. Coupons, tear-outs, folios, halftones — never web furniture.
  • Newspaper structure first, creativity layered inside it.
  • No fabricated testimony, statistics, or bylines. Real people only.
The design law
  • True white background, black ink, grays between. Colour comes from the content, never from decoration. No dark-first "AI tool" look — including in here.
  • Adrian makes the art. The system is the press: it places, it doesn't repaint.
