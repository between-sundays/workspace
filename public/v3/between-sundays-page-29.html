The Man Who Carried A Chair

A modern parable about a person who brought his own seat everywhere.
Room For One More

Reserved by fear

Available by welcome

He carried the chair through the grocery store first. People assumed he was returning it. At the bus stop he set it down, sat for three minutes, then picked it up again before the bus arrived.

By Wednesday everyone on his street had seen it: the blue folding chair, dented at the back leg, moving with him like a second shadow. When someone finally asked why, he said he was tired of arriving at places that had no room for him.

The person asking did not correct him. She only pulled another chair from her porch and set it beside his. For a moment he did not know what to do with the one in his hands.

Not a moral.A small story about room, welcome, and what someone carries because they are not sure there will be a place for them.
