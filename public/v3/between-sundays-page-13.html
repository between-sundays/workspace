Four chapters, one promise, twenty years

The Place Did
Not Change.Jacob's Sight Did.

He left the same ground he came back to. Nothing about Bethel was renovated between the first night and the last. What changed was a man who had been told, in the dirt, that he was not alone — and who lived long enough to find out it was true.

Genesis 27-28He runs. A stolen blessing behind him, nothing settled ahead.
Genesis 28:10-22He sleeps in a place with no name and wakes up somewhere else.
Genesis 31Twenty years on, God names the place again and tells him to go back.
Genesis 35He returns, puts away what he was carrying, and builds the altar.
