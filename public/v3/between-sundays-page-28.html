Letters From The Places Between

Found in a waiting room / 3:14 PM I was only looking for a magazine.

I found one sentence I kept rereading instead. It did not explain my whole week. It just made the room feel less empty.

Left on a bench / after lunch To whoever picks this up next:

I hope your day has one quieter place in it. If it does, I hope you notice before you rush past it.

Prompt for the next reader Where were you when a line found you?

Answer only if you want to. Blank space is allowed here.
