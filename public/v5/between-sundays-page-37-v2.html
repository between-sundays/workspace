Food / Page 37 / Daily Bread notes

A table is a place.

Food pages do not have to become sermons. They can do what good food writing already does: make you notice appetite, time, care, and the miracle of being fed.

For this issue, bread becomes less of a metaphor to explain and more of a trailhead to follow.

Read next: Matthew 5:6. Then ask what kind of hunger that verse names.

Try this: Invite one person to eat without making the meal productive.
