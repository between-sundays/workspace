The Geography of Nowhere

A working map of places people almost apologize for being in: airports, spare rooms, sidewalks, dark woods, and the quiet middle of a sentence.

Not every holy place announces itself first.
Genesis 28 starts with a traveler sleeping outside.

This issue follows Jacob before the ladder, before the altar, before the name Bethel meant anything to him. The point is not to over-explain the place. It is to notice it.
