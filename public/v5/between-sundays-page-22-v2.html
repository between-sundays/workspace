Obituaries for Things Not Dead

Certainty, beloved illusion.

Certainty lived loudly, demanded final answers, and left behind several unfinished conversations. In lieu of flowers, ask a better question.

The five-year plan, revised.

Known for its beautiful charts and stubborn confidence. Survived by the next faithful step.

Nowhere, renamed.

Once thought to be empty. Later discovered to have been carrying more presence than expected.

The perfect version of me.

No service will be held. Friends may gather as they are.

Found

A reason to stop calling every delay a waste.

Wanted

People willing to admit they are still learning.

Notice

"Surely Yahweh is in this place, and I didn't know it."
