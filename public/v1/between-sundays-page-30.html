Stones In The City

No speech balloons today.

The sidewalk declined comment.

Six quiet frames about noticing what is already underfoot.

Six quiet framesJoshua 4:6-7
01 / Curb He almost tripped over it.

A shoe stopped before the argument started.

02 / Blame He blamed the sidewalk.

The city was accused first. It did not defend itself.

03 / Silence The stone said nothing.

Which, to be fair, was very consistent.

04 / Look He looked down.

Not mystical. Just attention moving from speed to the thing underfoot.

05 / Hand He picked it up.

Same stone. Different distance.

06 / Pocket He kept it until he remembered.

Some objects become questions when they survive the walk home.

Not a moral.A tiny story about how a place changes when someone finally looks at it.
