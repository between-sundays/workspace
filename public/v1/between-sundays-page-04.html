From Page 03

Genesis does not introduce Jacob here as ready or impressive. The scene begins with a person asleep in a place he did not choose for beauty.

That is why the story has room for ordinary geography. Bethel is a particular place, but the repeated line is portable: "I am with you."

Some places feel fluorescent, unfinished, or painfully ordinary. This page is not trying to explain them away. It is a way to read Genesis 28 with those places in view.

Between Sundays returns to this because faith can disappear from view in the middle of logistics. The school pickup. The hospital hallway. The job search. The airport delay. The unread message. The long drive home.
