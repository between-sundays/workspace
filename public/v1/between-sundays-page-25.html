Maybe Here Counts Too

A short note for whoever is holding this.

You do not have to be in a church building to pause. You do not have to have perfect words. This page is simply an invitation to notice the place you are already in.

Place Check Waiting room Kitchen table Bus stop Car seat Laundry chair Somewhere you almost rushed past
Try this here. Look around the room you are actually in. Name one thing that is carrying you today. Read the line below without trying to solve your whole life.

"He is not far from each one of us."

Acts 17:27 / World English Bible
