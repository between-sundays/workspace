Obituaries For Things Not Dead

A formal goodbye to the things we keep reviving.

Featured Notice

The Need To Earn Rest

Longtime resident of tired bodies. Memorial donations may be made in naps.

Services will be held whenever the body is allowed to stop proving itself useful.

Control

Beloved by anxious calendars and five-year plans. Survived by surrender, trust, and several unfinished lists.

The Perfect Timeline

Passed quietly after repeated attempts to make life obey a spreadsheet.

Being Fine

Known for smiling at the wrong times. Services will be held whenever honesty arrives.

Old Shame

Frequently returned uninvited. No forwarding address will be provided.

Noise

A constant companion. Remembered by those who mistook volume for presence.

The Last Word

Survived by a quieter sentence that did not need to win.

Perfect Readiness

Delayed indefinitely after the road began anyway.

Comparison

Known for turning rooms into scoreboards. Memorials may be made by looking again.

Service Note

No flowers required. Bring one honest sentence, one loosened grip, and enough quiet to let the goodbye mean what it means.

Memorial Reading

"Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest."

Matthew 11:28 / World English Bible
Corrections Control has not actually died, but the family remains hopeful. Memorial Reading Matthew 11:28-30 is printed as an invitation, not a performance review.
