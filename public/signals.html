The prospecting brief

Every signal across every persona group, on one page. This is the document you hand an agent and say go find me a hundred people who match this — and the document a human reads before opening a single profile.

What a signal is, and isn't

A signal is something a person chose to publish — a caption, a tagged church, a bio line. It is never a guess, a demographic, or an inference from who someone is related to. There is no faith score here and there never will be. We record the post; we don't grade the person.

How to run it
  • Permitted search and human reading only. LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids scripts, bots and crawlers against profiles, and forbids automated messaging — so we search and read like a person. No scrapers, ever.
  • One person, one message, a real reason. No sequences, no volume sends.
  • Anything commercial by email carries accurate sender details and a working opt-out (CAN-SPAM applies to individual messages, not just blasts).
  • If a creator receives anything of value — free issues included — the relationship gets disclosed clearly in whatever they post. That's FTC guidance and it's also just the honest version.
  • An opt-out is permanent. Mark them do not contact and they're never surfaced again.
