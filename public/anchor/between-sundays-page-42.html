Work
Most of your life happens at a desk.
The Bible spends more time on people working than on people worshipping, which is not the impression most of us grew up with.
MONDAY
Colossians 3:23
The day most people decide their life is not working. Statistically the worst hour is 11am.
THE COMMUTE
Ephesians 5:16
Forty minutes nobody counts as part of your life. It is about a tenth of your waking week.
THE JOB YOU DID NOT PICK
Genesis 29:20
Jacob worked seven years for the wrong outcome, then seven more. Genesis reports it without comment.
THE ONE WHO NOTICES
Matthew 6:4
Every workplace has somebody holding it together quietly. It might be you and nobody has said so.
The line
“Work willingly at whatever you do, as though you were working for the Lord rather than for people.” COLOSSIANS 3:23 · NLT
