The stone he slept on
Verse 11
07  The Reading  1/7 Between Sundays  ·  Issue 001
Genesis 28  ·  New Living Translation
He stopped
for the night
because the sun
went down.
Jacob was not on a pilgrimage. He was running — away from a brother who wanted him dead, toward an uncle he had never met. He stopped where he stopped for one reason. It got dark.
Before you read

Nothing about this place was special. It had no name worth keeping. He did not choose it, pray about it, or build anything before he lay down. He used a stone for a pillow because a stone was what was there.

That is the whole setup — a man too tired to keep going, in a place he did not pick, on a night he did not plan. Read the next thirteen verses knowing that is where God turned up.
