A removable section
Seven days,
one chapter.
Lift these four pages out and keep them somewhere you will see them. One reading a day, each about a minute. Nothing to sign up for and nobody checking.
Day one
Genesis 28:10–11
He stops because it gets dark. Nothing spiritual happens yet.
Where did you stop today, and was it your choice?
Day two
Genesis 28:12
A ramp, with traffic going both ways, while he is asleep.
What might already be going on that you are not awake for?
Day three
Genesis 28:15
I am with you. I will not leave you until I have finished.
Read it as said to you. Write down what you resist about that.
Day four
Scripture: Genesis 28:10–22, NLT
