A word. A phrase. A thought you'd have written in a journal. You don't have to know what it's for — that's the point. Everyone else, people and agents, comes along and builds on it: what's the story, how far could it go, what's the modern parable, what do you see. Then it gets pulled into an issue, a product, a campaign.
