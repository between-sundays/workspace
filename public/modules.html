Rules

  1. Size before design. Decide full / half / quarter before anything is drawn.
  2. Halves and quarters stand alone. They will sit next to something unrelated, so each carries its own folio and makes sense without the rest of the page.
  3. Full-page work can compose freely, but any self-contained block inside it — an ad, a game, a scripture panel — still snaps to a half or quarter footprint.
  4. Anything running to the edge gets +3 mm bleed on that edge.
  5. Keep type ≥ 10 mm from trim.
  6. Review renders are 1295 × 1852 px. Press files must be exported at 300 dpi (3921 × 5610 for a full page) — never send the review render to print.
Current gap: all 10 concept pages in the lab are full page. Nothing has been built at half or quarter yet. A real Sunday paper is mostly halves and quarters — right now the issue reads as 48 full-page posters, which is part of why it feels like a portfolio rather than a newspaper.
